# Ji-Ching Chen (Livia) 

I am currently a PhD student at the Seismological Laboratory, California Institute of Technology (Caltech), CA, USA (2025-)

## Research Experience

**Research Assistant** — Institute of Earth Sciences, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan (2022–2024)
- Modeled temporal variations in Europa's ice shell thickness using a parameterized convection approach, exploring the effects of tidal heating, ice viscosity, and orbital eccentricity ([Europa_ice_shell](https://github.com/chingchenn/Europa_ice_shell), [pythonStagYY](https://github.com/chingchenn/pythonStagYY))
  - Published: *Temporal Changes in Europa's Ice Shell Thickness: Insights From Models of Convection*, Chen, Deschamps & Hsieh, **JGR: Planets**, 2026. DOI: [10.1029/2024JE008928](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024JE008928)
- Investigated the effect of thermal conductivity variations on subducting slab dynamics ([pythonStagYY_ThermalConductivity](https://github.com/chingchenn/pythonStagYY_ThermalConductivity))
- Advisors: Frédéric Deschamps and Wen-Pin Hsieh

**Graduate Student** — Department of Geosciences, National Taiwan University (2021–2022)
- M.S. thesis: *Origin and Evolution of Flat Slab Subduction: Insights From Numerical Geodynamics Modelling*
- Advisors: Eh Tan (Academia Sinica) and Shu-Huei Hung (NTU)
- Investigated the mechanical controls on flat slab subduction in Mexico and Chile using the FLAC (Fast Lagrangian Analysis of Continua) numerical package
- Analyzed slab geometry, stress distribution, plate forces, and magma generation using custom Python tools ([pythonFLAC](https://github.com/chingchenn/pythonFLAC), [geoflac](https://github.com/chingchenn/geoflac))
- Thesis repository: [ThesisNTU](https://github.com/chingchenn/ThesisNTU) | DOI: [10.6342/NTU202300033](http://dx.doi.org/10.6342/NTU202300033)
